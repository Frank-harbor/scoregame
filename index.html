    How to play

    Themes
    Hear half a second of a soundtrack and pick what it comes from. Game themes give you four to choose from, movie themes give you ten. Stuck? Skip to a longer clip: 1, 3, 5, then 10 seconds.
    Movies and Games
    Four titles, each with a hidden score. Pick the highest, or switch to Lowest. Hits, Popular and Everything set how well known the titles are.
    1v1
    Create a room and send the link. You both get the same choices and five lives. A wrong pick, or no pick in time, costs a life. Last one standing wins.
    Keys
    Number keys pick a card (0 is the tenth). Space plays the clip, S skips to a longer one, M mutes. Enter moves on.
